Duties

Record daily income and expenditure in the college’s books
Reconcile fee payments and issue receipts
Prepare payment vouchers and support the preparation of monthly reports
Keep financial records organised and secure
Assist with audits and any other duties assigned by the Head of Finance

Requirements

Diploma or Certificate in Accounting, Business, or a related field (CPA Section 3 or above is an added advantage)
Experience in bookkeeping or an accounts office, preferably in a public institution
Working knowledge of spreadsheets and accounting software
Honesty, accuracy, and good communication skills
