Mathioya Technical & Vocational College invites sealed tenders from eligible and qualified suppliers for the supply and delivery of computers and accessories for the ICT Department.

Obtaining tender documents
The tender document can be downloaded from this page using the attachment above, or collected from the Procurement Office, Mathioya Technical & Vocational College, Kamacharia, during office hours (Monday to Friday, 8am to 5pm).

Submission
Completed tenders must be enclosed in a sealed envelope marked “[reference number] SUPPLY AND DELIVERY OF COMPUTERS AND ACCESSORIES” and addressed to:

The Principal
Mathioya Technical & Vocational College
P.O. Box 363-10204, Kiria-ini

Tenders must be delivered or posted to reach the college by the closing date and time shown above.

Opening
Tenders will be opened on the date and time shown above in the presence of bidders or their representatives who choose to attend, at the college boardroom.

Enquiries
Email mathioyatti@gmail.com or call +254 790 678 738 (WhatsApp) during office hours.
